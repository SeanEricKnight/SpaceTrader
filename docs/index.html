A galaxy full of
good deals and bad ideas.

Start with a small ship, a little cash, and a map full of possibilities. Make your fortune one jump at a time.

Buy cargo where it’s plentiful. Sell where it’s scarce. Keep enough fuel to get home—and enough credits to repair whatever finds you out there.

  • Trade goods across a freshly generated galaxy
  • Plot reachable jumps and watch your fuel
  • Take courier jobs to fund the next leg
  • Face pirates, police, and hard choices

The captain’s toolkit

Four tabs. One big galaxy.

First playable port
⌁

Markets

Buy and sell ten goods. Local economies, changing stock, and a spread that rewards a patient trader.

◎

Galaxy

Explore 120 familiar system names in a newly generated connected map. Travel within range and one jump at a time.

⚒

Your ship

Refuel, repair, upgrade, and trade ships. Then see how long your latest financial decision survives.

✉

Commander

Take courier work, manage loans, read your log, and build toward the long-shot dream of retirement.

☄

Encounters

Fight, flee, or surrender when pirates arrive. Police inspections make contraband a calculated risk.

▣

Local save

Your progress is saved on device after actions and when the app leaves the foreground. One commander slot.
